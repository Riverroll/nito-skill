# Evidence labels

> Part of the `nito-accounting` skill. Text is taken from the research report dated 16 November 2026 (`research/erp-accounting-implementation-guide.md` in this repo). Every claim keeps its evidence label; see `evidence-labels.md`. Worked entries are constructed illustrations: amounts invented, rules sourced. Rates, caps and deadlines live in `values-to-verify.md` and must be re-checked before use.

## How to read the evidence labels

Every statement carries one grade. **STANDARD** is the text of an IFRS/IAS/PSAK or the Conceptual Framework; **LAW** is statute or regulation text; **GUIDANCE** is an IAI, DJP, BI or COSO publication; **TEXTBOOK** is OpenStax Principles of Accounting Vol. 1; **SECONDARY** is a vendor, news or professional-firm summary; **CODE** and **VENDOR** are open-source code or official product documentation; **CONSTRUCTED** is a worked example whose amounts were invented (the arithmetic was machine-checked in the notes, and the totals below were re-added when this report was written); **DESIGN INFERENCE** is a conclusion drawn across sources, named with the sources it rests on; **UNVERIFIED** means the notes could not open a primary source or found no source. Two access caveats affect the reading. The Conceptual Framework and IAS 1 text came from third-party copies carrying the IFRS Foundation footers because ifrs.org was login-walled for them ([Conceptual Framework copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493397/mod_resource/content/1/conceptual-framework-for-financial-reporting.pdf); [IAS 1 copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493402/mod_resource/content/1/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements.pdf)). IAS 7, IAS 8, IAS 21 and IFRS 10 came from the 2026 ifrs.org HTML edition, which already carries IFRS 18 consequential amendments, so some wording differs from the version in force for 2026 year-ends. No PSAK paragraph text was opened anywhere; PSAK rules below are IFRS rules mapped through IAI's numbering rule.
