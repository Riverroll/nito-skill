## Corporate income tax, final taxes and other cycles

The corporate rate is 22% from tax year 2022, 3 points lower for listed companies meeting the public-float conditions ([UU 7/2021 Pasal 17](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu7-2021bt.pdf), LAW). PwC adds a 50% rate discount on taxable income relating to turnover up to Rp4.8 billion for companies with turnover up to Rp50 billion, without citing the article, so that facility is SECONDARY ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/indonesia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)). The UMKM final tax of 0.5% of turnover up to Rp4.8 billion is described in a DJP deck on PP 20/2026 (5 June 2026), which removes the time limit for individuals and single-founder PTs, widens the turnover aggregation and adds a Pasal 20A bribe non-deductibility rule; the deck shows an application window of 22 April to 31 December 2026, but the PP text, its enactment date and the three-year limit for a PT (a Hukumonline snippet) were not opened ([DJP deck](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2026-06/Peraturan%20Pemerintah%20nomor%2020%20Tahun%202026.pdf)). Annual CIT and PPh 29 are due by the end of the fourth month, withholding taxes are paid by the 15th and filed by the 20th, and VAT is filed by month end, per PwC (reviewed 11 June 2026) and PMK 81/2024 summaries, both SECONDARY ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/indonesia/corporate/tax-administration); [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1806640/pmk-812024-terbit-coretax-seragamkan-tanggal-setor-pajak)). **A contradiction to carry forward:** PwC's resident withholding table lists interest at 0% and dividends at 15% for resident corporations, which does not match the Pasal 23 text (15% on interest unless paid to a bank, and domestic-company dividends are not taxable), so the skill follows the statute and flags a re-check ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/indonesia/corporate/withholding-taxes)). Fiscal correction inputs are non-deductible reserves, profit distributions, related-party excess payments, fines and bribes (positive) and final-taxed or exempt income (negative), with loss carry-forward of five years per PwC.

For multi-entity groups, IFRS 10 B86 requires combining like items, offsetting the investment against subsidiary equity and eliminating in full intragroup balances, transactions and unrealised profits ([IFRS 10](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ifrs10.html), STANDARD). The notes' CONSTRUCTED example: PT A sells goods costing 12,000,000 to PT B for 20,000,000 on credit and PT B has not resold. PT A books Dr Piutang Antar Perusahaan 20,000,000 / Cr Penjualan 20,000,000 and Dr HPP 12,000,000 / Cr Persediaan 12,000,000; PT B books Dr Persediaan 20,000,000 / Cr Utang Antar Perusahaan 20,000,000. On consolidation, **worksheet only** (never in either entity's ledger), Elim 1 is Dr Utang Antar Perusahaan 20,000,000 / Cr Piutang Antar Perusahaan 20,000,000, and Elim 2 is Dr Penjualan 20,000,000 / Cr HPP 12,000,000 and Cr Persediaan 8,000,000, leaving consolidated inventory at the group's original cost of 12,000,000. If PT B had resold everything for 28,000,000, Elim 2 is Dr Penjualan 20,000,000 / Cr HPP 20,000,000, leaving group sales of 28,000,000 and cost of sales of 12,000,000. Branch ledgers within one legal entity have no cited Indonesian rule, so the branch dimension recommendation is UNVERIFIED engineering practice.
