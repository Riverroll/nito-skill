## Multi-currency, rate sources and the Rupiah requirement

A foreign-currency transaction is recorded at the spot rate on the transaction date (a weekly or monthly average is allowed if rates do not fluctuate significantly), monetary items are retranslated at the closing rate with the difference in profit or loss, and non-monetary items at historical cost stay at the transaction-date rate (IAS 21.21-23, 21.28; STANDARD, [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias21.html)). Translation to a different presentation currency takes assets and liabilities at the closing rate, income and expenses at transaction-date rates, and the difference to OCI (IAS 21.39). A branch is part of the reporting entity but still a "foreign operation" if its currency differs (IAS 21.8). The notes found **no PSAK or IAI source that mandates JISDOR or BI's kurs tengah** for accounting, so "accounting uses JISDOR" is UNVERIFIED industry practice, while BI publishes JISDOR daily (Rp 18,250.00 on 16 November 2026 per the BI table read, [BI JISDOR](https://www.bi.go.id/id/statistik/informasi-kurs/jisdor/default.aspx)). Tax conversions differ: VAT on foreign-currency transactions uses the Minister of Finance's weekly kurs in effect on the date the tax invoice should have been issued, citing PP 44/2022 Pasal 21 ([DJP](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/artikel/transaksi-ppn-jasa-luar-negeri-dengan-dolar-pakai-kurs-kapan), GUIDANCE). A design inference follows: store per line the transaction currency amount, rate, rate source and date, and the Rupiah amount, with two rate series (accounting policy and KMK tax) and a separate, per-period, reversible revaluation run over open monetary items only. Bookkeeping in a foreign currency or language needs the Minister's permission (UU KUP Pasal 28; UU 8/1997 Pasal 8). DJP's summary of PMK 196/2007 lists eligible taxpayers (such as PMA companies and subsidiaries of overseas parents), a request at least 3 months before the fiscal year, a decision within one month and a minimum of five years, but PMK 196/2007 was reportedly amended three times, so those conditions are UNVERIFIED against current text ([DJP](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/tata-cara-penyelenggaraan-pembukuan-dengan-menggunakan-bahasa-asing-dan-satuan-mata-uang-selain)).

The cumulative FX result over X1-X3 is +1,000,000 (161,000,000 less 160,000,000), equal to +2,000,000 in 2026 and (1,000,000) in 2027. IAS 21.28 puts both realised and unrealised differences in profit or loss, so separating the accounts is a reporting convenience, not an IAS 21 requirement.
