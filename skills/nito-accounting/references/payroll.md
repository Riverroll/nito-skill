## Payroll: gross-to-net, BPJS, PPh 21, THR and severance

PPh 21 for permanent employees under PMK 168/2023 (in force from 1 January 2024) is monthly gross times a TER rate for January to November, with the last period a full-year Pasal 17 computation less tax already withheld. The TER category follows PTKP status (A: TK/0 and TK/1 and K/0; B: TK/2, K/1, TK/3, K/2; C: K/3), non-employees are taxed at gross x 50% x the Pasal 17 rate, and employer-paid JKK and JKM premiums and employer-borne PPh 21 are added to gross ([DJP deck on PMK 168/2023](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2024-02/PMK%20168%20Tahun%202023%20Tentang%20PPh%20Pasal%2021%20TER.pdf), GUIDANCE; [JDIH summary](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-168-tahun-2023/summary)). The full TER A, B and C bracket tables (44, 40 and 41 brackets) are in the DJP deck and belong in the skill's dated-parameter file, not in prose; the PMK body, the biaya jabatan cap, the treatment of an employer-paid health premium and the rounding rule were not opened. The statutory Pasal 17 progressive rates are 5% to Rp60 million, 15% to Rp250 million, 25% to Rp500 million, 30% to Rp5 billion and 35% above, with PTKP of Rp54 million plus Rp4.5 million for marriage and per dependent up to three ([UU 7/2021](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu7-2021bt.pdf)).

BPJS Ketenagakerjaan contributions in the regulations opened are JHT 5.7% (2% employee, 3.7% employer; [PP 46/2015](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp46-2015bt.pdf)), JP 3% (1% employee, 2% employer) on a wage base capped at Rp7 million in 2015 and indexed yearly by prior-year GDP growth ([PP 45/2015](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp45-2015bt.pdf)), and employer-paid JKK of 0.24%, 0.54%, 0.89%, 1.27% or 1.74% by risk class and JKM 0.30% ([PP 44/2015](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp44-2015bt.pdf)), payable by the 15th with a 2% monthly fine. **Two items conflict and must not be presented as settled.** The 2026 JP cap is about Rp10,547,000 on BPJS's own page dated 17 December 2025 ([BPJS](https://www.bpjsketenagakerjaan.go.id/artikel/18913/artikel-berapa-besaran-iuran-jht-jkk-jkm-jp-dan-jkp)) but Rp11,086,300 from 1 March 2026 in secondary sources ([Talenta](https://www.talenta.co/blog/iuran-jaminan-pensiun-terbaru/)); the ratio of 1.0511 is consistent with an indexation of about 5.1%, but the GDP figure was not verified. The JKK rates were later reclassified (BPJS cites PP 6/2025, another source PP 7/2025, and a DPR copy of PP 6/2025 shows a different "very low" figure of 0.10%), so the employer's class rate should come from its BPJS invoice. BPJS Kesehatan at 1% employee plus 4% employer with a Rp12 million wage ceiling is reported from Perpres 64/2020 only through search summaries (UNVERIFIED). THR is one month's wage after 12 months of service, pro rata from one month, payable at least seven days before the holiday, with a 5% late fine (Permenaker 6/2016 via pasal.id, status "not reviewed"; [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/en/peraturan/permen/permenakertrans-no-6-tahun-2016)). Severance multipliers by years of service are in PP 35/2021 Pasal 40 (pesangon up to 9 months of wage at 8 years or more; penghargaan masa kerja up to 10 months at 24 years or more), but the multipliers by termination reason were not read ([PP 35/2021](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp35-2021bt.pdf)). IAS 19 requires short-term benefits recognised as service is rendered, so THR and leave are accrued monthly, while severance and pension need an actuarial estimate ([IAS 19](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-19-employee-benefits/)).
