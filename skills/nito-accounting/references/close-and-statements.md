## Close, locking and the statements derived from the ledger

A complete set of statements is a statement of financial position, profit or loss and OCI, changes in equity, cash flows and notes, with comparatives and, where policy is applied retrospectively or items are restated, a third position statement (IAS 1.10, 1.36, 1.38; STANDARD, [IAS 1 copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493402/mod_resource/content/1/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements.pdf)). UU 40/2007 Pasal 66 lists the same components for a PT's annual report, due to the RUPS within six months of year-end, prepared under SAK, with a public-accountant audit required in the Pasal 68 cases; pasal.id warns that UU 11/2020, Perppu 2/2022 and UU 6/2023 may have amended these articles, so the current consolidated text is UNVERIFIED ([Pasal 66](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-40-tahun-2007/pasal-66); [Pasal 68](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-40-tahun-2007/pasal-68), LAW via a secondary host). The Indonesian statement titles (Laporan Posisi Keuangan; Laporan Laba Rugi dan Penghasilan Komprehensif Lain; Laporan Perubahan Ekuitas; Laporan Arus Kas; Catatan atas Laporan Keuangan) are verified only for SAK EP ([IAI SAK EP slides](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_berita/Materi%20Presentasi_Sosialisasi%20SAK%20Entitas%20Privat.pdf)). The IAS 1.54 minimum position-statement lines run from property, plant and equipment through issued capital and reserves, current classification follows IAS 1.60 and 1.66 (the liquidity-presentation exception and IAS 1.69 liability criteria were not read), and operating cash flow may be reported by the direct or indirect method (IAS 7.18; the quoted wording is from the IFRS 18-amended 2026 edition, [IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias7.html)).

The notes' tie-out (CONSTRUCTED, from E1-E19) shows the derivation. The position statement shows current assets 553,500,000 (Bank 444,000,000; receivables net 49,000,000; inventory 49,500,000; prepaid rent 11,000,000) plus equipment net 23,500,000 for total assets of **577,000,000**, equal to liabilities 66,000,000 plus equity 511,000,000. Profit for January is 82,000,000 less 71,000,000 = **11,000,000**. Equity moves from 0 by capital 500,000,000 and profit 11,000,000 to 511,000,000. The indirect cash flow is profit 11,000,000, plus non-cash 2,000,000, less working-capital movements of 45,000,000 (receivables 50,000,000, inventory 50,000,000, prepaid rent 11,000,000 up; payables 60,000,000, accrued salaries 3,000,000, unearned revenue 3,000,000 up), giving operating cash flow of (32,000,000); the direct method gives receipts 35,000,000 less 40,000,000, 15,000,000 and 12,000,000 paid, also (32,000,000). Adding investing (24,000,000) and financing 500,000,000 yields net cash of 444,000,000, equal to Bank. The design consequences, all inferences, are that the position statement reads adjusted balances, the profit statement reads period movements (not cumulative balances) for a mid-year month, the cash-flow statement reads movements of non-cash balance-sheet accounts classified operating, investing, financing or non-cash, and any posting that bypasses the ledger breaks the tie-out.

The month-end checklist is CONSTRUCTED from the cycle: cut-off, subledgers reconciled to control accounts, bank reconciliations, unadjusted trial balance, adjusting entries (accruals, deferrals, depreciation, allowance, inventory, FX revaluation, tax accruals), adjusted trial balance, intercompany matching, statements, maker-checker review, period lock, and at year-end closing entries and opening balances equal to closing balances. Soft versus hard close, a 13th adjustment period and the mechanics of period locks have **no standard or law behind them** in the notes; they are ERP or audit practice. The fiscal-year rules that are sourced: the tax year is one calendar year unless the taxpayer keeps a different book year, bookkeeping follows consistency on an accrual or cash basis, and changing the method or fiscal year needs DJP approval ([UU 28/2007 Pasal 1](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-28-tahun-2007/pasal-1) and [Pasal 28](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-28-tahun-2007/pasal-28), LAW via a secondary host; ayat numbering may differ in the official text after UU HPP 7/2021). PER-8/PJ/2025 regulates the procedure and DJP's decision is due 15 working days after electronic receipt, per a DDTC snippet only ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1811289/per-8pj2025-atur-perubahan-metode-pembukuan-apa-yang-berubah), SECONDARY, UNVERIFIED).
