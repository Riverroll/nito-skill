Open the ledger

Every part of the books.

  • ChartA 29-account Indonesian template. The engine reads type and flags, never the code.
  • JournalsAdjusting, reversing, closing and correcting entries. Nothing posted is ever edited.
  • AR and APOpen items that tie to the control account, PPN Keluaran and Masukan, PPh 23.
  • Stock and assetsValuation and COGS, GR/IR, depreciation commercial and fiscal, leases.
  • Cash and bankA journal per account, register payment from the invoice, fees, transfers, reconciliation.
  • PayrollBPJS, PPh 21 with TER, THR and severance.
  • ClosePeriod locks, multi-currency revaluation, the statements derived from the ledger.
  • Any ERPA playbook and domain maps connect a CRM that only invoices, supply chain, voyage and shipping, projects. The dashboard comes with it.

Install.

As a plugin, from inside Claude Code. Both skills come with it.

/plugin marketplace add Riverroll/nito-skill
/plugin install nito-skill

Or copy one skill into your own skills folder:

git clone https://github.com/Riverroll/nito-skill.git
cp -r nito-skill/skills/nito-accounting ~/.claude/skills/

They apply on their own. To call one by name, type /nito-design or /nito-accounting.
