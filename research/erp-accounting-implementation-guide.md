# Build one accounting core, swap only configuration

An Indonesian ERP team can implement accounting correctly by treating the ledger as a small, chart-agnostic core (balanced header-and-line journals, immutable once posted, corrected only by reversal, with every line carrying a Rupiah amount and a source reference) and pushing everything that varies into data: the chart of accounts, the accounting standard applied, the costing method, PKP status, effective-dated tax parameters, the fiscal year and the currency policy. The rules that make a ledger correct are old and well sourced (**debits equal credits per entry, accrual basis, corrections by new entries, trial balance as a check**) and the Indonesian overlay is concrete: PSAK/ISAK were renumbered from 1 January 2024, SAK EP replaced SAK ETAP from 1 January 2025, PSAK 118 (IFRS 18) applies from 1 January 2027, books must be kept in Rupiah, Latin letters and Indonesian for **10 years** unless the Minister of Finance permits otherwise ([IAI](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Perubahan_Penomoran_PSAK_ISAK_dalam_SAK_Indonesia.pdf); [UU 8/1997](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu8-1997.pdf)). Real systems (Odoo, ERPNext, Oracle, Dynamics) all route subledger documents through one accounting layer into header-and-line ledger rows, and neither open-source system studied (Odoo, ERPNext) enforces "debits = credits" with a database constraint. On MariaDB/MySQL that invariant, gapless numbering and immutability must live in application code inside one transaction, because a CHECK is single-row, subqueries are not allowed in it, deferred constraints exist only in NDB, and AUTO_INCREMENT leaves gaps ([MySQL 8.4 CHECK](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/create-table-check-constraints.html); [AUTO_INCREMENT](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/innodb-auto-increment-handling.html)). The recommended packaging, which is a **design inference** and not a documented pattern of any named system, is a `nito-accounting` skill in the owner's `nito-skill` repo with a router `SKILL.md`, one reference file per topic, a company-configuration schema, a machine-readable dated-parameter file and a posting contract that every other module must call. Many tax and payroll parameters are secondary-sourced, in conflict between sources or unverified (for example the 2026 JP wage cap and the JKK rates), so they are kept in one dated table (the "Values to re-verify" section) and never hard-coded; every worked journal entry below is a **constructed illustration** whose amounts are invented and whose rule is sourced.

## How to read the evidence labels

Every statement carries one grade. **STANDARD** is the text of an IFRS/IAS/PSAK or the Conceptual Framework; **LAW** is statute or regulation text; **GUIDANCE** is an IAI, DJP, BI or COSO publication; **TEXTBOOK** is OpenStax Principles of Accounting Vol. 1; **SECONDARY** is a vendor, news or professional-firm summary; **CODE** and **VENDOR** are open-source code or official product documentation; **CONSTRUCTED** is a worked example whose amounts were invented (the arithmetic was machine-checked in the notes, and the totals below were re-added when this report was written); **DESIGN INFERENCE** is a conclusion drawn across sources, named with the sources it rests on; **UNVERIFIED** means the notes could not open a primary source or found no source. Two access caveats affect the reading. The Conceptual Framework and IAS 1 text came from third-party copies carrying the IFRS Foundation footers because ifrs.org was login-walled for them ([Conceptual Framework copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493397/mod_resource/content/1/conceptual-framework-for-financial-reporting.pdf); [IAS 1 copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493402/mod_resource/content/1/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements.pdf)). IAS 7, IAS 8, IAS 21 and IFRS 10 came from the 2026 ifrs.org HTML edition, which already carries IFRS 18 consequential amendments, so some wording differs from the version in force for 2026 year-ends. No PSAK paragraph text was opened anywhere; PSAK rules below are IFRS rules mapped through IAI's numbering rule.

## The core ledger: five invariants, one Indonesian framework choice, and a chart that serves the statements

### Foundations and the rules the ledger must enforce

The ledger rests on the accounting equation and on the Conceptual Framework's five elements. An asset is "a present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events", a liability is "a present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource", equity is the residual, and income and expenses are changes in assets and liabilities that change equity other than through owner contributions and distributions (CF 4.3, 4.26, 4.63, 4.68, 4.69; STANDARD, [Framework copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493397/mod_resource/content/1/conceptual-framework-for-financial-reporting.pdf)). Financial statements are prepared on the accrual basis except for cash-flow information, on a going-concern assumption assessed over at least twelve months, without offsetting unless a standard permits (IAS 1.25-27, 1.32; STANDARD, [IAS 1 copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493402/mod_resource/content/1/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements.pdf)). A journal is the book of original entry, each entry has at least one debit and one credit, and "the dollar value of the debits must equal the dollar value of the credits" ([OpenStax 3.5](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/3-5-use-journal-entries-to-record-transactions-and-post-to-t-accounts), TEXTBOOK). A trial balance lists all non-zero ledger balances, the two columns must agree, and it catches only computational errors ([OpenStax 3.6](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/3-6-prepare-a-trial-balance)). The notes did not open the textbook list of errors a trial balance cannot detect, so the statement that it cannot detect wrong-account or wrong-period postings is a design inference from "computational errors", not a sourced list.

| Account type (Indonesian) | Normal balance | Increases with | Statement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset (Aset) | Debit | Debit | Position |
| Liability (Liabilitas/Utang) | Credit | Credit | Position |
| Equity (Ekuitas) | Credit | Credit | Position |

Its practical consequence is that "debit" is a position, not an increase: a debit to Utang Usaha reduces a liability, so a ledger that stores one signed amount must define the sign against the normal balance, or store separate debit and credit columns ([OpenStax 3.2](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/3-2-define-and-describe-the-expanded-accounting-equation-and-its-relationship-to-analyzing-transactions)).

### Which Indonesian standard applies is a configuration fact, not a constant

IAI's DSAK approved the Kerangka Standar Pelaporan Keuangan Indonesia (KSPKI) on 12 December 2022, effective 1 January 2024. It defines four pillars: SAK Internasional, SAK Indonesia (now called SAK Umum), SAK for Entitas Privat (EP, effective 1 January 2025, replacing SAK ETAP) and SAK EMKM, with Sharia PSAK/ISAK layered on top where relevant. Entities with public accountability use Pillar 1 or 2, entities without it use Pillar 2 or 3, and a move to a lower-requirement pillar is not permitted ([IAI KSPKI deck, Nov 2023](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_berita/Materi%20Sosialisasi%20KSPKI%20dan%20Nomenklatur_Nov2023.pdf), GUIDANCE; the deck states it carries the speaker's personal views, not IAI's). SAK EP is an adoption of IFRS for SMEs (2015) with 35 chapters, and an entity with public accountability may use it only where the competent authority allows ([IAI SAK EP slides](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_berita/Materi%20Presentasi_Sosialisasi%20SAK%20Entitas%20Privat.pdf), GUIDANCE, discussion material). The SAK EMKM facts (approved 24 October 2016, effective 2018, minimum statements are position, income statement and notes) are SECONDARY search-result summaries only ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/apa-itu-sak-emkm-33179)) and its primary text and size thresholds were not opened. The owner's group is probably Pillar 2 or 3, but that is a facts question (public accountability, prior framework, lender or regulator requirements), so the design inference is to make the framework a company setting and keep the ledger identical, since the differences are mostly measurement and disclosure.

PSAK/ISAK were renumbered effective 1 January 2024 with "no change in the content" ([IAI](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Perubahan_Penomoran_PSAK_ISAK_dalam_SAK_Indonesia.pdf)). The first digit is 1 for an IFRS-derived standard, 2 for an IAS-derived one, 3 for local, 4 for Sharia, and the last two digits are the IFRS/IAS number. ### Journals, posting, adjusting, closing and correcting, with balanced worked entries

Journal anatomy is: date, debit lines first, credit lines after, a description and a reference to an original source document; posting moves the lines to the general ledger, and the cycle runs identify, journal, post, unadjusted trial balance, adjusting entries, adjusted trial balance, statements, closing entries, post-closing trial balance and optional reversing entries ([OpenStax 3.3/3.5](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/3-5-use-journal-entries-to-record-transactions-and-post-to-t-accounts); [OpenStax 5.1](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/5-1-describe-and-prepare-closing-entries-for-a-business)). Companies using special journals (sales, purchases, cash receipts, cash disbursements) post monthly totals to the general ledger while subsidiary ledgers hold per-customer and per-supplier detail ([OpenStax 7.2](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/7-2-describe-and-explain-the-purpose-of-special-journals-and-their-importance-to-stakeholders)); the notes did not confirm the phrase "control account" in the text and inferred the concept. Adjusting entries pair one income-statement account with one balance-sheet account and never touch cash ([OpenStax 4.3](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/4-3-record-and-post-the-common-types-of-adjusting-entries)). Closing moves revenue, expenses and dividends to retained earnings through an Income Summary, and the post-closing trial balance must show only permanent accounts ([OpenStax 5.1](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/5-1-describe-and-prepare-closing-entries-for-a-business)). Corrections of material prior-period errors are restated retrospectively (IAS 8.41-42, [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias8.html), STANDARD); IAS 8 governs statements, and "never edit a posted entry" is an internal-control principle, not a stated legal rule.

### Close, locking and the statements derived from the ledger

A complete set of statements is a statement of financial position, profit or loss and OCI, changes in equity, cash flows and notes, with comparatives and, where policy is applied retrospectively or items are restated, a third position statement (IAS 1.10, 1.36, 1.38; STANDARD, [IAS 1 copy](https://moodle2.units.it/pluginfile.php/493402/mod_resource/content/1/ias-1-presentation-of-financial-statements.pdf)). UU 40/2007 Pasal 66 lists the same components for a PT's annual report, due to the RUPS within six months of year-end, prepared under SAK, with a public-accountant audit required in the Pasal 68 cases; pasal.id warns that UU 11/2020, Perppu 2/2022 and UU 6/2023 may have amended these articles, so the current consolidated text is UNVERIFIED ([Pasal 66](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-40-tahun-2007/pasal-66); [Pasal 68](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-40-tahun-2007/pasal-68), LAW via a secondary host). The Indonesian statement titles (Laporan Posisi Keuangan; Laporan Laba Rugi dan Penghasilan Komprehensif Lain; Laporan Perubahan Ekuitas; Laporan Arus Kas; Catatan atas Laporan Keuangan) are verified only for SAK EP ([IAI SAK EP slides](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_berita/Materi%20Presentasi_Sosialisasi%20SAK%20Entitas%20Privat.pdf)). The IAS 1.54 minimum position-statement lines run from property, plant and equipment through issued capital and reserves, current classification follows IAS 1.60 and 1.66 (the liquidity-presentation exception and IAS 1.69 liability criteria were not read), and operating cash flow may be reported by the direct or indirect method (IAS 7.18; the quoted wording is from the IFRS 18-amended 2026 edition, [IAS 7](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias7.html)).

### Multi-currency, rate sources and the Rupiah requirement

A foreign-currency transaction is recorded at the spot rate on the transaction date (a weekly or monthly average is allowed if rates do not fluctuate significantly), monetary items are retranslated at the closing rate with the difference in profit or loss, and non-monetary items at historical cost stay at the transaction-date rate (IAS 21.21-23, 21.28; STANDARD, [IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ias21.html)). Translation to a different presentation currency takes assets and liabilities at the closing rate, income and expenses at transaction-date rates, and the difference to OCI (IAS 21.39). A branch is part of the reporting entity but still a "foreign operation" if its currency differs (IAS 21.8). The notes found **no PSAK or IAI source that mandates JISDOR or BI's kurs tengah** for accounting, so "accounting uses JISDOR" is UNVERIFIED industry practice, while BI publishes JISDOR daily (Rp 18,250.00 on 16 November 2026 per the BI table read, [BI JISDOR](https://www.bi.go.id/id/statistik/informasi-kurs/jisdor/default.aspx)). Tax conversions differ: VAT on foreign-currency transactions uses the Minister of Finance's weekly kurs in effect on the date the tax invoice should have been issued, citing PP 44/2022 Pasal 21 ([DJP](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/artikel/transaksi-ppn-jasa-luar-negeri-dengan-dolar-pakai-kurs-kapan), GUIDANCE). A design inference follows: store per line the transaction currency amount, rate, rate source and date, and the Rupiah amount, with two rate series (accounting policy and KMK tax) and a separate, per-period, reversible revaluation run over open monetary items only. Bookkeeping in a foreign currency or language needs the Minister's permission (UU KUP Pasal 28; UU 8/1997 Pasal 8). DJP's summary of PMK 196/2007 lists eligible taxpayers (such as PMA companies and subsidiaries of overseas parents), a request at least 3 months before the fiscal year, a decision within one month and a minimum of five years, but PMK 196/2007 was reportedly amended three times, so those conditions are UNVERIFIED against current text ([DJP](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/tata-cara-penyelenggaraan-pembukuan-dengan-menggunakan-bahasa-asing-dan-satuan-mata-uang-selain)).

### Controls, retention law and the common errors they prevent

Indonesian law requires bookkeeping in good faith, in Indonesia, in Latin letters, Arabic numerals, Rupiah and Indonesian, recording assets, liabilities, capital, income, costs, sales and purchases, with books, records and supporting documents (including electronic data) kept 10 years at the place of business or corporate seat (UU KUP Pasal 28 as rendered by [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-28-tahun-2007/pasal-28); the DDTC quote of Pasal 28(11) is a snippet, [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/literasi/kelas-pajak/16241/kewajiban-pembukuan-dan-pencatatan)). UU 8/1997 independently defines "catatan" to include annual statements, accounts and "jurnal transaksi harian", requires Rupiah and Indonesian, and keeps financial records 10 years from the end of the fiscal year (Pasal 5, 8, 11) while Pasal 10(1) requires the annual statements on paper and Pasal 10(2) lets accounts and journals be kept on other media ([UU 8/1997](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu8-1997.pdf), LAW, read in full). Because the daily journal is itself a retained legal record, deleting posted entries is both a control failure and a retention problem (inference). Whether later electronic-transaction laws changed the paper requirement, and whether cloud hosting outside Indonesia conflicts with the in-Indonesia rule, were not researched and need counsel.

The ledger controls the notes derive are: balanced entries per entry (OpenStax 3.5); no edits or deletes of posted rows, with corrections by reversal and re-post; unique sequential numbering with gap detection; an audit trail retained 10 years; segregation of duties and maker-checker ([OpenStax 8.2](https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/8-2-define-and-explain-internal-controls-and-their-purpose-within-an-organization)); period locks; a Rupiah amount on every line; and an exact-decimal money type. **Only the first and the Rupiah and retention items have a legal or standard source; gapless numbering, the audit-log shape, maker-checker thresholds and period-lock behaviour are engineering controls** and the notes found no Indonesian accounting law requiring gapless journal numbers (Faktur Pajak numbering is a separate tax rule). COSO's framework (1992, updated 2013, five components) is cited only for existence; the component and principle names were not read from COSO ([COSO](https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic)), so any list from memory is UNVERIFIED. The common bugs, each tied to the rule they violate in the notes, are: treating debit as increase; checking balance only on the trial balance; storing running balances without journal lines; recording cash movement as the transaction in an accrual ledger; editing posted entries; using floats for money; a single signed amount with no sign convention; posting directly to control accounts; leaving revenue or expense balances after closing; retranslating non-monetary items; skipping period-end remeasurement of monetary items; netting receivables against payables without permission (IAS 1.32); using old PSAK numbers; assuming one framework; and putting the KMK tax rate in the accounting FX field. A validator list of eight checks follows from these (inference): balanced in transaction and functional currency; account active and postable; period open; mandatory dimensions present; unique document number; approver different from maker; no update or delete on posted rows; FX rate source and date recorded.

## Subledgers and Indonesian tax: the same ledger, six transaction cycles

### Order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and the PPN overlay

AR is the customer subledger whose total must equal the control account Piutang Usaha, and revenue follows the five-step IFRS 15 model (identify the contract and obligations, determine and allocate the price, recognise revenue when control transfers) ([IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/), STANDARD, summary page only; paragraph references such as IFRS 15 para 35 are UNVERIFIED). The notes' central design rule, an inference, is to keep three dates on every sale: delivery or control transfer (revenue, COGS, stock out), Faktur Pajak (PPN Keluaran) and cash. Faktur Pajak is issued at delivery, at receipt of an advance, at each instalment, or at export, and advance receipts in one month can be combined into a "Faktur Pajak Gabungan" ([DJP](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/artikel/cara-membuat-faktur-pajak-uang-muka-dan-pelunasan-di-coretax-djp), GUIDANCE, citing PER-11/PJ/2025 Pasal 31-32; DJP does not give the offset arithmetic, so the final-invoice example follows common practice and is an inference). Customers that are withholding agents deduct PPh 23, so a receipt short by 2% of the DPP is the normal signature, and the bukti potong is a prepaid-tax asset rather than a revenue reduction (inference).

The tax treatment of credit losses matters for the ledger: a bad debt is deductible only if expensed in the commercial accounts, listed to DJP, and supported by one of the court, agreement, publication or debtor-acknowledgement conditions (UU PPh Pasal 6(1)(h) as amended by UU 7/2021, [UU 7/2021](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu7-2021bt.pdf), LAW); reserve funds are not deductible except listed ones (Pasal 9(1)(c)), so an expected-credit-loss allowance of an ordinary trading company is a positive fiscal correction and a temporary difference. The implementing PMK 105/2009 and its threshold are UNVERIFIED (snippets only).

On the purchase side, AP is the supplier subledger tied to Utang Usaha with a three-way match feeding a goods-received-not-invoiced account. The three-way-match tolerances have no regulatory source and must be policy parameters. PPN Masukan is credited against output of the same period (Pasal 9(2)), is not creditable when there is no direct business link or the Faktur Pajak is defective (Pasal 9(8)b, 9(8)f), may be credited up to three Masa Pajak later if not expensed or capitalised (Pasal 9(9)), and excess input carries forward or is refunded in defined cases (Pasal 9(4), 9(4a), 9(4b)) ([UU 7/2021](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu7-2021bt.pdf)). Withholding on vendor payments is PPh 23 (2% on services and rent of assets other than land and buildings; 15% on dividends, interest, royalties and prizes; 100% higher without NPWP, [UU 36/2008](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu36-2008bt.pdf), the 2008 text with later amendments unchecked for this article), final PPh 4(2) on building rent and construction, PPh 26 on non-residents, and PPh 22 on imports. The construction final rates (1.75%, 2.65%, 4%; consulting 3.5%, 6%; integrated 2.65%, 4%) are mapped in a DJP deck citing PP 51/2008 as amended by PP 9/2022 ([DJP deck](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2026-07/Aspek%20Perpajakan%20Usaha%20Jasa%20Konstruksi.pdf), GUIDANCE).

### Inventory, fixed assets, cash and bank

IAS 2 measures inventories at the lower of cost and net realisable value, using specific identification for non-interchangeable items and FIFO or weighted average for interchangeable items ([IAS 2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-2-inventories/), STANDARD, summary page; its LIFO prohibition is stated only in secondary summaries). ## How real ERPs implement the ledger, and what MariaDB can and cannot enforce

### Ledger shape, money types and the posting layer

All compared systems use a header-and-line model or a voucher-keyed line table, with a base-currency amount and a foreign-currency amount on every line. Odoo 17 stores `account.move` and `account.move.line`, with a signed `balance` from which `debit` and `credit` are derived in the company currency, plus `amount_currency` and `currency_id`, and a SQL constraint `check_credit_debit` (credit times debit equals zero) so a line is one side only ([Odoo account_move_line.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py), CODE). ERPNext's GL Entry keeps separate debit and credit plus account-currency and transaction-currency columns, a `voucher_type`, `voucher_no` and `voucher_detail_no` source link, and an `is_cancelled` flag ([ERPNext gl_entry.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/gl_entry/gl_entry.json), CODE). Oracle Fusion's `GL_JE_LINES` has one-to-many headers to lines, separate entered and accounted debit and credit columns and a conversion rate ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/25d/oedmf/gljelines-20303.html), VENDOR). Oracle's `GL_JE_HEADERS` columns, SAP's BKPF/ACDOCA fields and the schemas of Tryton, OFBiz, GnuCash and NetSuite were **not verified**. SAP's Universal Journal is known only from SAP Learning and a SAP PRESS blog: ACDOCA combines general ledger, controlling, asset accounting and the material ledger, and BKPF/BSEG still exist for open-item management ([SAP Learning](https://learning.sap.com/courses/outlining-cost-management-and-profitability-analysis/discussing-the-concept-of-the-universal-journal_f62cf151-5562-44f5-8ff6-df16fb232e94); [SAP PRESS](https://blog.sap-press.com/what-is-saps-universal-journal)), with the claim that aggregate tables were removed resting on search snippets (UNVERIFIED against help.sap.com).

Posting is a separate layer. Oracle's Subledger Accounting turns an accounting event plus transaction data and rules (journal-line, description and account rules inside an accounting method) into subledger journal entries, then summarises or details them into the GL; "transaction objects need to be populated before the accounting for the events occurs" ([Oracle Accounting Hub guide](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E36909_01/fusionapps.1111/e20374/F484500AN2530D.htm), VENDOR). Dynamics 365 Finance builds subledger journal entries from posting profiles or priority-ordered posting definitions, requires balanced debits and credits, transfers to the ledger in detail or summary, and uses the voucher as the shared reference ([Dynamics ledger-subledger](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/ledger-subledger); [posting definitions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/posting-definitions), VENDOR). ERPNext funnels everything through `make_gl_entries`, which handles dimension offsets, period and disabled-account checks, merging of similar entries, and a debit-credit difference check that either raises "Debit and Credit not equal" or posts a small difference to a Round Off account ([general_ledger.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/accounts/general_ledger.py), CODE); its Company doctype carries more than twenty default-account fields (bank, receivable, payable, round-off, write-off, realised and unrealised FX, inventory, stock adjustment, accumulated depreciation, CWIP, advances, deferred revenue and expense, and others) ([company.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/setup/doctype/company/company.json)). Odoo's l10n_id chart template sets property accounts (receivable `l10n_id_11210010`, payable `l10n_id_21100010`, stock accounts), FX gain and loss accounts, 8-digit codes and default taxes `tax_ST4` and `tax_PT4` ([template_id.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/l10n_id/models/template_id.py), CODE). Account type drives behaviour rather than the number: Odoo's `account_type` keys (asset_receivable, liability_payable, equity_unaffected and others) set report placement and the `reconcile` flag, and ERPNext's `root_type`, `report_type` and `account_type` do the same ([account_account.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/account_account.py); [account.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/account/account.json)).

Money types differ, and the notes flag that. DECIMAL is MySQL's documented exact type for monetary data; ISO 4217 gives IDR an exponent of 2 (JPY 0, KWD 3) ([MySQL 8.4](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/fixed-point-types.html); [SIX list-one](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/financial-information/data-center/iso-currrency/lists/list-one.xml)); that the sen is "no longer used in practice" is from Indonesian Wikipedia only, with no Bank Indonesia source ([id.wikipedia](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupiah)). TigerBeetle uses unsigned 128-bit integer minor units ([TigerBeetle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tigerbeetle/tigerbeetle/main/docs/coding/data-modeling.md)); Odoo's `stock.valuation.layer.unit_cost` is a Float ([stock_valuation_layer.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/stock_account/models/stock_valuation_layer.py)); Prisma maps `Decimal` to decimal.js with a MySQL default of DECIMAL(65,30) ([Prisma](https://www.prisma.io/docs/orm/reference/prisma-schema-reference)), and decimal.js defaults to 20 significant digits and ROUND_HALF_UP ([decimal.js](https://mikemcl.github.io/decimal.js/)). Floats fail because 0.1 has no finite binary representation, with a documented single-precision compound-interest error of 1.40 dollars ([Goldberg](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E19957-01/806-3568/ncg_goldberg.html)) and JavaScript's safe integer limit is 2^53 - 1 ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Number/MAX_SAFE_INTEGER)). Both Odoo (ROUND(SUM) to the currency's decimal places) and ERPNext (small differences to a Round Off account) resolve line-versus-total drift with a rounding tolerance and a dedicated account, which supports the design inference of one rounding point. That `new Prisma.Decimal(24.454545)` from a JS number inherits float imprecision is not in the Prisma docs and is an inference; construct from strings. Fowler's `allocate` method and Dinero.js allocation were not verified.

### Immutability, numbering, locking and reconciliation in practice

Odoo enforces balance in Python: `_check_balanced` runs SQL grouped by move with `HAVING ROUND(SUM(line.balance), decimal_places) != 0` inside create and write ([Odoo account_move.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/account_move.py), CODE). It uses states draft, posted and cancel, blocks deleting posted lines, reverses rather than deletes entries that cannot be unlinked, and offers an optional SHA-256 hash chain per journal over name, date, journal, company and lines with a `secure_sequence_number`, which becomes mandatory for French companies where cancellation and modification methods are deactivated ([Odoo France docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/finance/fiscal_localizations/france.html), VENDOR). ERPNext keeps originals and posts matching reversals on cancellation; the setting name "Enable Immutable Ledger" comes only from a search snippet ([ERPNext](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/immutable-ledger-in-erpnext)). The high-scale published ledgers agree: Square Books uses only inserts and corrects by new entries with balances cached on entries ([Square](https://developer.squareup.com/blog/books-an-immutable-double-entry-accounting-database-service/)); Modern Treasury keeps posted entries permanent and balances derived from aggregate fields with optimistic `lock_version` ([Part II](https://moderntreasury.com/journal/how-to-scale-a-ledger-part-ii); [optimistic locking](https://moderntreasury.com/journal/designing-ledgers-with-optimistic-locking)); Stripe's Ledger is an immutable event log, uses clearing accounts that must net to zero as a completeness test and requires two-phase review for corrections ([Stripe](https://stripe.dev/blog/ledger-stripe-system-for-tracking-and-validating-money-movement)); Helland's "Accountants don't use erasers" gives the principle ([CIDR 2015](https://www.cidrdb.org/cidr2015/Papers/CIDR15_Paper16.pdf)); Fowler's Accounting Transaction sums to zero and prefers Reversal Adjustment when entries are immutable ([Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/AccountingTransaction.html)). No source documents the anti-pattern of deriving the GL from operational tables at report time, and the notes found no property-based test suite for ledgers.

Numbering is where the systems and the owner's stack diverge most. Odoo numbers per journal using a max-plus-one scan with retry on a PostgreSQL unique violation, backed by a **partial unique index** on `(name, journal_id)` for posted entries, and by its own code it does not guarantee gapless numbers ([sequence_mixin.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/sequence_mixin.py), CODE). Both MySQL AUTO_INCREMENT and PostgreSQL sequences leave gaps after rollback ([MySQL](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/innodb-auto-increment-handling.html); [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-sequence.html)). Period locks are application hooks everywhere (Odoo `_check_fiscalyear_lock_date` with optional irreversible `account_lock`; ERPNext Accounting Period and Accounts Frozen Upto; [Odoo year-end](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/finance/accounting/reporting/year_end.html), [ERPNext Accounting Period](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/accounting-period)), with LedgerSMB's PostgreSQL trigger `prevent_closed_transactions` the only database-level lock found ([triggers.sql](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ledgersmb/LedgerSMB/master/sql/modules/triggers.sql)). Closing models differ: Odoo computes reports in real time and treats the closing entry as optional with an `equity_unaffected` account type, whereas ERPNext's Period Closing Voucher moves profit and loss to equity but "does not erase history or stop backdated entries" ([ERPNext PCV](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/period-closing-voucher)). Balance storage has three documented strategies: computed from lines (Odoo), a per-period summary table (Oracle's `GL_BALANCES` with begin balance and period net debit and credit) and cached running balances (Square, Modern Treasury, TigerBeetle) ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/25d/oedmf/glbalances-25110.html)), and any cache is a derived copy of the lines that needs a rebuild job and a reconciliation to SUM(lines) (inference from Helland).

Reconciliation structures differ by design. Odoo matches open items with `account.partial.reconcile` (debit and credit move, amount, `full_reconcile_id`, `exchange_move_id`) ([account_partial_reconcile.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/account_partial_reconcile.py)); ERPNext uses a Payment Ledger Entry ([payment_ledger_entry.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/payment_ledger_entry/payment_ledger_entry.json)) and a Stock Ledger Entry with `valuation_rate`, `stock_value_difference` and a FIFO `stock_queue` ([stock_ledger_entry.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/frappe/erpnext/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/stock_ledger_entry/stock_ledger_entry.json)); ERPNext's goods-received-not-invoiced account is "Stock Received But Not Billed", credited at Purchase Receipt and debited by Purchase Invoice ([ERPNext perpetual inventory](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/perpetual-inventory)). Multi-currency follows the same pattern: Odoo posts exchange differences to a dedicated journal and accounts ([Odoo multi-currency](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/finance/accounting/get_started/multi_currency.html)), ERPNext runs an Exchange Rate Revaluation that needs an unrealised gain/loss account and supports reversal journals ([ERPNext](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/exchange-rate-revaluation)); where Odoo implements unrealised revaluation was not located. On Indonesia, Odoo 17 splits the localisation into `l10n_id` (chart and taxes), `l10n_id_efaktur` (CSV export and a 13-digit number format) and a master-branch `l10n_id_efaktur_coretax` module ([Odoo Indonesia docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/finance/fiscal_localizations/indonesia.html)), and DJP's Coretax import uses XML with converters (Faktur Keluaran converter v1.6 dated 23 January 2026, field names unverified) ([DJP](https://www.pajak.go.id/reformdjp/coretax/template-xml-dan-converter-excel-ke-xml)). ERPNext's `id_chart_of_accounts.json` exists among 76 verified charts but its contents were not opened, and Frappe's marketing claim of PPN, PPh, e-Faktur and BPJS support is unverified.

### The enforcement boundary for MariaDB, MySQL and Prisma

| Rule | Can the database enforce it? | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Exact money values | Yes, with DECIMAL(p,s) | [MySQL fixed-point](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/fixed-point-types.html) |
| A line is debit or credit, never both; non-negative amounts | Yes in principle: a row-level CHECK on the same row (Odoo's `credit * debit = 0` is the model); enforcement version on MySQL 8.0.x and MariaDB behaviour not confirmed | [MySQL 8.4 CHECK](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/create-table-check-constraints.html); [MariaDB constraint](https://mariadb.com/kb/en/constraint/) |
| Sum of debits equals sum of credits per entry | **No.** CHECK is single-row, "Subqueries are not permitted", other tables not allowed; only NDB defers FK checks | MySQL 8.4 CHECK and [FK](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/create-table-foreign-keys.html) pages |
| Same enforcement via triggers | **Limited.** A trigger cannot modify a table already used by the invoking statement; SIGNAL is permitted; MariaDB trigger behaviour not opened | [MySQL stored-program restrictions](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/stored-program-restrictions.html) |
| Gapless document numbers | **No with AUTO_INCREMENT** (gaps after rollback, not reused); yes with a locked counter row via `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` in the posting transaction | [MySQL AUTO_INCREMENT](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/innodb-auto-increment-handling.html); [locking reads](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/innodb-locking-reads.html) |
| Unique number per journal and company | Yes with a unique index; Odoo's partial unique index (posted only) is a PostgreSQL feature and MySQL partial-index absence was not verified in docs | [Odoo account_move.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/account/models/account_move.py) |
| Immutability of posted rows | Not documented as a DB feature on MySQL; Odoo, ERPNext, Square and Modern Treasury do it in application code or by never issuing UPDATE; a DB trigger guard is a possible design but is untested in the notes | CODE and ENG sources above |
| Period lock | Not at DB level except LedgerSMB's PostgreSQL trigger; other systems use application hooks | [LedgerSMB](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ledgersmb/LedgerSMB/master/sql/modules/triggers.sql) |
| Referential integrity of lines to header and accounts | Yes, with FKs (InnoDB checks immediately). Partitioned InnoDB tables cannot have FKs, so partitioning a ledger table conflicts with FKs | [MySQL partitioning limits](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/partitioning-limitations-storage-engines.html) |
| Atomicity and isolation | Yes: transactions; InnoDB default REPEATABLE READ; under READ COMMITTED locking reads lock only index records, so phantoms can occur | [MySQL isolation](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/innodb-transaction-isolation-levels.html) |

Prisma's `$transaction` accepts an `isolationLevel` (default is the database's), an interactive-transaction `timeout` defaulting to 5 seconds and `maxWait` of 2 seconds ([Prisma reference](https://www.prisma.io/docs/orm/reference/prisma-client-reference)); how it behaves on MariaDB beyond these options was not verified. The synthesis the notes support, and which is **a design inference** from the MySQL, Odoo and ERPNext sources and not a documented pattern of any named system, is a single `$transaction` that (a) locks a per-journal counter row with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, (b) inserts header and lines with DECIMAL amounts, (c) asserts that the line sum is zero in the same transaction and in both transaction and functional currency, and (d) marks the entry posted, followed by a verification query and a scheduled job asserting `SUM(debit) = SUM(credit)` globally. Idempotency should come from a client-generated key stored uniquely, as in Stripe (keys can be pruned after at least 24 hours, parameters compared on retry; [Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests)), TigerBeetle's transfer `id` and the transactional-outbox pattern whose relay may publish more than once, so consumers must be idempotent ([microservices.io](https://microservices.io/patterns/data/transactional-outbox.html)).

### The posting contract

The contract is the single write path. A module never inserts ledger rows; it submits a **posting request** to the accounting service, which validates it, applies account determination from configuration and writes balanced lines in one transaction, returning the journal number. This follows ERPNext's `make_gl_entries` as the single write path, Oracle's event-then-rules-then-journal flow and Dynamics' posting profiles, and it carries the source reference that all four systems store on lines.

| Contract field | Meaning | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| `company_id`, `legal_entity` | Ledger owner | Dynamics (one ledger per legal entity), ERPNext |
| `idempotency_key` | Unique per posting request; a replay returns the first result and a parameter mismatch is an error | Stripe, TigerBeetle `id`, outbox |
| `event_type` | From the event catalogue below | Oracle SLA event |
| `source_type`, `source_id`, `source_line_id` | Document that caused the posting, copied onto every line | ERPNext `voucher_type`, `voucher_no`, `voucher_detail_no`; Dynamics voucher |
| `posting_date`, `document_date`, `tax_point_date` | Three dates: revenue or stock event, document, tax event | subledger notes (three dates on a sale) |
| `currency`, `transaction_amount`, `rate`, `rate_source`, `rate_date` | Per line; the service computes the Rupiah amount | IAS 21, Odoo and ERPNext line fields |
| `party_type`, `party_id` | Counterparty, drives control-account subledger | ERPNext party, Odoo partner |
| `lines[]` | Business amounts by role (for example `revenue`, `tax_output`, `receivable`), not account numbers | account determination |

## Conclusion

The most useful finding is that the hard problems are not the accounting rules. Debits-equal-credits, accrual, immutability and reversal are settled and stable; what moves is the data around them (PPN effective rate under a statutory 12%, the JP cap, JKK classes, PTKP, TER, construction and import rates, Faktur numbering and deadlines), and several of those are in open conflict in the sources the team will actually find. That argues for treating every rate as an effective-dated row with a status column, as `params-dated.json` does, and for a test that fails when prose states a rate the file does not contain. It also reframes the team's risk: the legal sources for the control rules that engineers assume are mandatory (gapless numbering, period locks, maker-checker, soft and hard close) do not exist in the notes, so those are defensible engineering controls, not compliance requirements, and the guide should say so every time.

On the owner's stack, the practical consequence is that correctness lives in one place: a single posting transaction that locks a counter row, writes DECIMAL lines, asserts that the sum is zero and marks the entry posted, behind a contract no module can bypass. MariaDB cannot be asked to carry the balance invariant, and the notes' own evidence is that neither Odoo nor ERPNext asks its database to either. What remains unresolved and should be queued for a follow-up pass: PSAK paragraph text, the PMK 72/2023 asset annex, the PMK 168/2023 body, Perpres on BPJS Kesehatan, the current UU KUP consolidated text, the choice of accounting FX rate source, MariaDB trigger and CHECK behaviour, and SAP's documented numbering and account determination.
